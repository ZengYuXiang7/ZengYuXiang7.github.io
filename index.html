🧑‍🎓About Me个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student at the College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University, supervised by Prof. Kun Xie. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Shantou University in 2024. I joined Hunan University in 2024 as a master's student in Electronic Information (Computer Technology) and continued into the doctoral stage in 2025.

My research focuses on Deep Learning and Data Mining. I welcome discussions and collaborations on related research topics. Feel free to contact me at zengyuxiang@hnu.edu.cn.

我是曾渝翔，湖南大学计算机学院计算机科学与技术专业硕博连读博士研究生，导师为谢鲲教授。我于 2024 年获得汕头大学计算机科学与技术专业学士学位，同年进入湖南大学攻读电子信息（计算机技术）硕士，2025 年转入硕博连读博士阶段。

我的研究方向是深度学习与数据挖掘。欢迎围绕相关研究问题与我交流合作，联系邮箱：zengyuxiang@hnu.edu.cn。
